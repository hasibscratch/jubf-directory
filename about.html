About JU Bankers' Forum

A community of Jahangirnagar University alumni united by their service in Bangladesh's financial sector.

🎯

Our Mission

To foster a strong, supportive community among JU alumni who are working in the banking and financial sector — facilitating networking, knowledge sharing, and mutual professional growth across all levels and institutions.

🌅

Our Vision

To be the most well-connected alumni network in Bangladesh's banking sector — a forum where the JU spirit of academic excellence continues to drive professional innovation and integrity in every financial institution our members serve.

📖

Our History

The JU Bankers' Forum was founded by a group of passionate JU alumni who recognized the growing presence of Jahangirnagar graduates in Bangladesh's banking sector. What began as an informal WhatsApp group has grown into a structured, registered alumni forum with members across central banks, commercial banks, MFIs, and development finance institutions nationwide.

🏛️

Our Activities

We organize annual reunions, professional development seminars, mentorship programs for current JU students entering the banking sector, blood donation campaigns, and social welfare activities — keeping the JU spirit alive long after graduation.
